Changes made by MapSurfe: numeric elevation grids are recolored using a
deep blue ocean / turquoise shelf / green-to-earth-tone elevation palette.
Matte relief shading is calculated from elevation gradients with key and fill
lighting; the colors do not represent reconstructed vegetation or climate.
Published snapshots can be cached as lossless PNG images.
At published ages the source numeric grid is used. At intermediate ages,
MapSurfe linearly interpolates the neighboring elevation grids on the GPU
before assigning land/ocean colors and relief shading. The resulting single
height surface uses zero elevation as its coastline; colored images are not
cross-faded. Displayed ages and the source bracket are identified in the app.

Intermediate maps are calculated visualizations made by MapSurfe, not
independent published scientific reconstructions. This geographic-grid height
interpolation does not separately model plate motion, erosion or sea level;
land may emerge or disappear over large intervals between source grids.

A 0.1-degree grid does not mean that every ancient valley is known.
Precambrian coastlines, relief and continental configurations remain uncertain.
